Preface

Project LIMEN is a sci-fi fantasy concept artbook centering around "Pharos", a city built around a central lighthouse where mysterious incidents have taken place.

This artbook was created as part of Senior Project, exploring original character and concept designs.

A bastion of light against the abyss.
